# Privacy Policy

**BG3 Mod Helper** does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

## Data stored locally

The following data is saved to your local machine only (`%LocalAppData%\BG3ModHelper\`):

- **API keys** (Nexus Mods, mod.io) — entered by the user, stored locally, used solely to communicate with the respective APIs on the user's behalf
- **Mod metadata and tracking records** — pak filenames, file IDs, download history — used for update detection and mod management within the app

None of this data is shared with the developer or any third party.

## Community UUID contributions

When a mod is downloaded or identified through the app, an anonymous mapping of the mod's UUID to its Nexus Mods file ID may be submitted to a community database. This data contains no personal information — only game file identifiers.

## Third-party services

The app communicates directly with:
- **Nexus Mods API** — subject to the [Nexus Mods Privacy Policy](https://www.nexusmods.com/about/privacy/)
- **mod.io API** — subject to the [mod.io Privacy Policy](https://mod.io/privacy)
